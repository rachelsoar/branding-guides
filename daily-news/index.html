Overview

Brand identity

Your town lost its paper. You can bring it back.

Daily News brings a daily local paper back to towns that lost theirs. Each paper has a human editor, works from the public record, and links every story to its source. The brand should look like a real paper people trust, made by people in their town: warm paper, black ink, one strong dark color and a few bright accents, used with restraint.

Logo

The mark

A paper airplane folded from a newspaper: local news, sent out to the town. It has three parts: the paper edge (the light halo that makes it read as newsprint), the body and the fold. The mark is used on its own, without the name.

Red has one job.

Text on a Red fill is On Red (#030301, 4.9:1); regular Ink is only 4.2:1. Red as text on Paper only at 24px and up (3.8:1). On Plum and Ink, switch to Red on dark. Keep Red away from Green (same value), Yellow and Sky; put Paper or Ink between them.

The color marks also use tints and shades that appear only inside the logo files: #706A5B (fold), #DDEBFF, #5181BE, #9BB555, #2B3F00, #FFF1D0, #BF9A34, #EFEFEB. Don't use them elsewhere.

Pairings

Pairings that work

All pass WCAG 2.2 AA. "All text" is 4.5:1 or better. "Large text" is 3:1 or better: 24px and up, icons and rules. Buttons keep the homepage CTA shape, "Check my town", and the hard Ink shadow; the fill changes with the ground. On Ink grounds the shadow turns Paper. Buttons are never Ink-filled: use a Paper button with a 2px Ink outline instead. Scores sit under each card: the pairing first, then the button.

Illustration & Styling

Illustration and styling

A newspaper folded into a paper airplane, with printed columns visible, against a blue sky. Use for heroes, ads, covers and announcements.

It's an illustration, not the logo: never shrink it to logo size or put it where the mark belongs. New illustrations follow the same idea: ordinary newsprint objects, shown simply. No faces, no glossy AI-art look.

Voice & Tone

How Daily News sounds

Plain and direct, like a good local editor. Talk to "you," about "your town."

Language we use (live on daily-news.ai)

Your town lost its paper. You can bring it back.

The AI newsroom handles the hours of reading a small paper can't staff anymore. You decide what runs.

We do the legwork. You do the journalism.

The paper didn't die because nobody cared. It died because nobody could afford the newsroom.

Every story links to its source. · One editor per town.

StepCopy
